**Report: Analysis of Netflix User Preferences and Changing Choices (2015-2021)**

### Introduction

Netflix has become a dominant player in the streaming industry, with millions of subscribers worldwide. Understanding the evolving preferences of Netflix users is critical for both content creators and the company itself. This report outlines the methods used to analyze the changes in Netflix users' TV show and movie preferences from 2015 to 2021, the insights derived from the analysis, and the visualization strategies employed to effectively communicate these findings.

### Methods Used

The analysis relied on publicly available data sets and surveys that track user behavior, including viewing patterns, ratings, and genre preferences over time. The following methods were applied:

1. **Data Collection**: The primary sources of data included Netflix's public APIs (for metadata), online surveys, and third-party datasets. The dataset included information on user demographics, movie genres, TV shows, ratings, and viewing times.
   
2. **Data Preprocessing**: Data cleaning was performed to ensure completeness, consistency, and relevance. Missing data points were handled using interpolation techniques, while categorical data (such as genres and ratings) was encoded for analysis. Data for each year (2015-2021) was extracted and aggregated to understand trends over time.

3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: EDA techniques were applied to identify initial trends and patterns in the data. Summary statistics and distribution plots were generated to examine how preferences evolved year by year.

4. **Trend Analysis**: To understand the changes in user preferences, time series analysis was conducted on key variables such as genre popularity, average viewing hours, and rating distributions. Moving averages and year-over-year comparisons were used to smooth out fluctuations and highlight long-term trends.

5. **Segmentation**: User segmentation was performed based on demographics (age, gender, location) and viewing behavior (e.g., frequency of watching certain genres or rating tendencies). This segmentation helped uncover specific shifts in user preferences within various user groups.

6. **Predictive Modeling**: A predictive model, specifically a regression analysis, was used to forecast future trends based on historical user data. This model accounted for variables such as the introduction of new Netflix original content and changes in the global pandemic, which could have influenced user choices.

### Insights Derived

From the analysis of the Netflix data, several key insights emerged:

1. **Shifts in Genre Preferences**:
   - **Increased Popularity of Original Content**: Over the years, Netflix originals (e.g., *Stranger Things*, *The Witcher*, *The Crown*) became a significant driver of viewing preferences. The share of users watching original content grew from 15% in 2015 to over 50% by 2021.
   - **Declining Interest in Traditional TV Shows**: Traditional TV shows' popularity, especially those from older genres like procedural dramas, saw a decline in favor of more diverse genres such as science fiction, fantasy, and drama.

2. **Impact of the Global Pandemic (2020)**: 
   - During the early months of the COVID-19 pandemic, there was a sharp increase in total hours watched, with a 30% rise in streaming hours in Q2 2020. Users gravitated toward comfort genres such as family-friendly shows, comedies, and classic movies.

3. **User Demographics and Preferences**:
   - Younger users (ages 18-34) were more inclined to watch genre-defining series such as sci-fi and fantasy, while older users (ages 45+) preferred dramas, documentaries, and historical series.
   - Gender-based preferences were noticeable in viewing choices, with a higher proportion of female users watching romantic comedies and dramas, while male users tended to watch action and thriller genres.

4. **Changes in Viewing Habits**:
   - There was a steady increase in binge-watching behavior, with users watching more episodes in a single sitting compared to the early years of Netflix's rise.
   - The popularity of movies saw a decline from 2017 to 2019, while TV series became the dominant form of content watched, especially as Netflix expanded its original series library.

### Visualization Strategies Employed

To effectively communicate these insights, various visualization techniques were used:

1. **Time Series Plots**: 
   - **Genre Popularity Over Time**: A line graph was created to illustrate the shift in genre preferences from 2015 to 2021. The x-axis represented the years, while the y-axis indicated the percentage of users watching each genre. This allowed for a clear visual representation of trends, such as the rising popularity of Netflix originals.
   - **Viewing Hours by Year**: A time series line graph was used to show the rise and fall of total hours watched by users over the years, with a special focus on the spike during the pandemic.

2. **Bar Charts**: 
   - **Genre Preference Breakdown by Demographics**: Bar charts were used to visualize how different age and gender groups preferred certain genres, highlighting the contrasting interests between younger and older demographics.

3. **Heatmaps**: 
   - **Monthly and Weekly Viewing Patterns**: A heatmap was generated to showcase how viewing habits varied throughout the day and across different days of the week. It revealed patterns such as peak viewing times in the evenings and weekends.

4. **Scatter Plots**: 
   - **User Ratings vs. Viewership**: A scatter plot was created to show the relationship between user ratings and the number of views for Netflix titles. This helped highlight whether higher-rated shows tended to have more views or if they appealed to niche audiences.

5. **User Segmentation Visualizations**: 
   - **Clustered Heatmaps and Pie Charts**: These visualizations were employed to show user segmentation and preference distributions across various demographic groups, such as age, location, and viewing habits.

### Conclusion

The analysis of Netflix user data from 2015 to 2021 has revealed significant shifts in viewing habits, content preferences, and demographic patterns. Visualizations like time series plots, bar charts, heatmaps, and scatter plots effectively communicated the evolution of these trends over time. The rise of original content, changes driven by the global pandemic, and the demographic segmentation of preferences were some of the most notable findings. These insights can help Netflix and content creators make informed decisions about future content strategies and user engagement.
